Asking good questions before a renovation starts prevents most of the misunderstandings that make renovations stressful. Here's a checklist to work through — some for yourself, and some for your renovator.

## Questions to ask yourself

- What problem am I trying to solve?
- What would make this renovation a success in two years' time?
- What's my rough budget range, and what's my contingency?
- What matters most if trade-offs are needed — finish, timing or cost?
- How will we live in the house while work is underway?

## About scope

- What exactly is included in the work, and what isn't?
- Who supplies fixtures, appliances and finishes?
- Is the scope documented in writing?
- What happens if I want to change something part-way through?

## About trades and responsibility

- Which parts of the work will you do personally?
- Which other trades are involved, and are they licensed for that work?
- Who coordinates the trades and the order of work?
- Who do I contact with questions during the project?

## About approvals

- Does this work need a building permit?
- Does the project need a registered builder, given its scope and value?
- Are there planning or heritage considerations for my property?
- Who arranges any approvals?

Your local council or a registered building surveyor can confirm what applies. Licences and registrations can be checked with the relevant Victorian regulator.

## About unexpected issues

- What happens if you find something unexpected behind a wall or under a floor?
- How are variations priced and agreed?
- How much contingency would you suggest for a home of this age?

## About timing

- When could you realistically start?
- What affects how long the project will take?
- Are there product lead times I should know about now?

## About communication

- How often will we catch up during the project?
- How do you prefer to be contacted?
- How will decisions be recorded?

## About the finish

- Can I see examples of similar finished work?
- How do you handle the final details and handover?
- What maintenance will the finished work need?

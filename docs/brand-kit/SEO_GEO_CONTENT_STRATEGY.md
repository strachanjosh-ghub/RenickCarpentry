# Renick Carpentry — Local SEO + GEO (AI Search) Content Strategy
Updated: 9 November 2026 | Geelong and Surf Coast, Victoria

## Core positioning
Quality Home Renovations — Thoughtfully planned, beautifully finished.
Alister is a hands-on local craftsman who both executes supplied plans and helps homeowners refine ideas based on what suits the home, practicality, budget and project feasibility. He can coordinate relevant trade specialists in his network. Avoid positioning as a volume builder, architect, general handyman, fence/gate installer, or designer of services beyond verified qualifications.

## Demand methodology and confidence
The following are priority keyword *hypotheses based on search intent and local search language*. They are NOT measured search-volume rankings. No access was provided to Google Keyword Planner, Google Search Console, Ahrefs, Semrush or Trends for this domain. Validate volume, competitiveness, actual queries and geography using those sources once available; do not claim 'most searched' from this plan alone.

## On-page templates
One descriptive H1 per page; concise HTML title and natural meta description; early locality and service context; link to related service and project; clear visible contact details; descriptive image filenames/alt text; short scannable questions that answer real customer concerns.
Example home title: Quality Home Renovations Geelong & Surf Coast | Renick Carpentry
Example services title: Bathroom Renovations Geelong | Quality Finishes | Renick Carpentry
Intro: "Renick Carpentry delivers thoughtfully planned home renovations across Geelong and the Surf Coast. Alister works directly with homeowners to shape practical ideas and deliver finishing details they're proud to live with."
Avoid keyword repetition, autogenerated suburb doorway pages, misleading credentials, unverified reviews, fabricated price ranges, or AI-generated 'completed projects'.

## Local business discovery / maps
Create or claim ONE Google Business Profile using exact verifiable registered name, primary category and legitimate service-area configuration; do not list a residential/street address publicly unless it genuinely qualifies and consent is given. Add service descriptions, real work photos, business hours, accurate contact info. Seek authentic reviews from completed clients without incentives; respond thoughtfully. Keep NAP (name, address if public, phone) consistent across website and credible listings. Consider local chamber/trade associations and supplier referrals if genuine.

## GEO = generative engine optimisation / AI discovery
AI results do not have a separately guaranteed optimisation switch. Improve discoverability and attribution with clear answer-first paragraphs, entity consistency (Alister, business name, Geelong, Surf Coast, real services), real documented project stories, detailed photo captions, honest credentials, quotes with permission, accessible HTML, standard structured data and crawlable public pages. Answer natural-language questions directly, with sourceable facts and no unsupported superlatives. No SEO tactic can guarantee AI citation.

## Suggested natural-language Q&A on service pages
- Who can manage a quality home renovation in Geelong?
- Can a renovation carpenter help refine my ideas before plans are final?
- How do I choose finishes that suit an older Surf Coast home?
- Can Renick coordinate plumbing and electrical trades as part of a renovation?
- What should I decide before renovating a bathroom?
- Can I retain the character of an existing home while updating a kitchen?
Answers must accurately reflect Al's practice and any applicable licence or contract boundaries.

## Project case study proof checklist
Permission to photograph / suburb or approximate area approved / building type / scope / main constraint / Al's contribution / coordinated trade roles / 3–8 real images / photo alt / before-after / timeline only if factual / client-approved quote / link to service / location.

## 90-day publishing plan
Weeks 1–2: audit contact details, Google Business Profile, domain canonicalisation, Search Console + GA4, launch core pages and 3 real projects.
Weeks 3–4: service-specific bathroom/kitchen pages, add real images and internal links.
Month 2: outdoor living service page; publish 2 project stories and 2 high-value planning articles; request reviews ethically.
Month 3: refresh pages using Search Console real queries; publish 2 more project stories; improve page load and enquiry friction; check AI-search referral patterns without treating them as definitive rank measures.

## Measurement
Organic impressions/clicks per page and query, enquiries/calls from organic, qualified lead rate, Search Console coverage, GBP actions, image performance, 3 months rolling. Report actual verified keyword demand, not assumptions. Avoid expecting immediate ranking guarantees.

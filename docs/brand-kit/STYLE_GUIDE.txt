CONTRAST PRINCIPLE
Never place the forest-green body of the R directly on dark forest green; use the separately supplied REVERSED version, with warm-cream body and timber features. This was specifically corrected after earlier sign/logo versions lacked contrast.
Primary dark-ground logo: joinery-R-reversed-dark. Primary light-ground logo: joinery-R-primary-light.
Use warm cream for all small text on dark green; gold only for large highlights and rules. Do not place gold as small print on cream backgrounds.

TYPOGRAPHY
Headings: Cormorant Garamond Semibold (suggested, obtain licence/font via Google Fonts or substitute Georgia Bold); body: Montserrat Medium (suggested, obtain from authorised source) or Arial.
Headings 44–64 px desktop / 32–40 px mobile; body 17–19 px / line-height 1.55; navigation minimum 16 px; fence sign use bold legible lettering readable at driving speed; phone number needs strongest visibility after company name/service.

LOGO CONSTRUCTION AND USAGE
Option A's geometric R is reconstructed as editable SVG: dark green upper bowl with timber vertical and diagonal features, a diamond joinery junction, cream negative-space counter. On dark ground the green becomes cream for legibility.
Preserve clear space at least 1/4 of emblem width; no shadows, gradients, squashing, or alternate colours. The logo should not be used at tiny dimensions with timber-grain pinstripes; use one-colour art for embroidery and tiny icons.
SVG for scalable production. PNG for CMS upload. Print vendor should verify actual strokes, colours, crop/bleed, materials before fabrication.
